💊 PillSYNC – AI-Powered Medication Management System

## 📖 About the Project

PillSYNC is an AI-powered healthcare mobile application developed using Flutter to improve medication adherence and simplify the daily medication management process.

The application combines modern mobile development technologies with Artificial Intelligence to provide users with an intelligent assistant capable of organizing medications, analyzing prescriptions, sending smart reminders, tracking medication adherence, and locating nearby pharmacies.

Unlike traditional medication reminder applications, PillSYNC integrates OCR and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to automatically recognize medication information from prescriptions or medicine packages, reducing manual data entry and minimizing human errors.

The project was developed as a Graduation Project at October 6 University and focuses on creating a complete healthcare ecosystem that connects mobile technologies, AI services, secure backend APIs, and location-based services into one intelligent platform.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Millions of patients forget to take their medications on time or struggle to manage multiple prescriptions.

Manual medication management often leads to:

- Missing medication doses.
- Incorrect dosage schedules.
- Medication errors.
- Poor treatment adherence.
- Difficulty locating nearby pharmacies.
- High medication costs due to lack of alternative suggestions.

PillSYNC aims to solve these problems through intelligent automation powered by Artificial Intelligence.
